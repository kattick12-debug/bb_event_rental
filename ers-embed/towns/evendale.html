What our customers say

Good questions

Frequently asked

Does the price include delivery and setup?

Yes, within 30 miles. Extra fees may apply farther out, up to 40 miles. Prices don't include sales tax.

How much space do I need?

Most bouncers are 13' × 13' and most combos are 31' × 13'. Some water slides are up to 20' × 45'. Add about 5' on every side. Check your yard with Will It Fit →

Can I have a bounce house at a park in Evendale?

Sharon Woods, a Great Parks of Hamilton County park next door, allows inflatables with Great Parks' approval at least 15 business days ahead (no water slides, and no electricity, so add a generator). We haven't confirmed the rules for Evendale's village parks, so check with the village first. Always confirm with the park before you book. See local parks that allow bounce houses →

Do I need power?

Yes, an outlet within 100 feet. Most parks don't have electricity, so you can rent a generator from us.

What's your deposit and cancellation policy?

A $50 card deposit holds your date. It's fully refundable if you cancel 8 or more days out. Cancel 2–7 days out and you get a rain check good for a year.

How do I pay?

Cash or credit card. If you pay cash, have exact change ready because drivers don't carry change.
