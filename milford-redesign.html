Good questions

Frequently asked

Does the price include delivery and setup?

Yes, within 30 miles. Extra fees may apply farther out, up to 40 miles. Prices don't include sales tax.

How much space do I need?

Most bouncers are 13' × 13' and most combos are 31' × 13'. Some water slides are up to 20' × 45'. Add about 5' on every side. Try our yard checker →

Do I need power?

Yes, an outlet within 100 feet. Most parks don't have electricity, so you can rent a generator from us.

What's your deposit and cancellation policy?

A $50 card deposit holds your date. It's fully refundable if you cancel 8 or more days out. Cancel 2–7 days out and you get a rain check good for a year.

How do I pay?

Cash or credit card. If you pay cash, have exact change ready because drivers don't carry change.
