Planning checklist

Planning a big event?

A few things to sort out early so setup day goes smoothly. We'll walk through all of it with you.

  • 1
    PowerEach inflatable needs its own outlet within 100 ft. Fields and parks often have none, so we can bring 4,550 W or 10,000 W generators.
  • 2
    SpaceFlat ground with about 5 ft clear around each unit. Grass is best; dirt, asphalt and concrete work too.
  • 3
    VolunteersPut an adult at every unit to manage the line and the safety rules. Our larger courses list 1–2 attendants each.
  • 4
    PermissionUsing a park or school grounds? Get approval in writing. Many local parks don't allow inflatables; see which ones do.
  • 5
    WeatherCancel 8+ days out for a full refund; 2–7 days out gets a rain check good for a year.
